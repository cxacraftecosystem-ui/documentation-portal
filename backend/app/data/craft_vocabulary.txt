# Craft vocabulary sent to the speech-to-text providers as boosted terms (see
# app/services/ai.py, craft_keyterms). These are the words a general model mishears in a field
# interview: technique names, dye and material names, local tool names, and the villages the
# crafts are named after. Everything here was taken from the crafts, products and tools already
# recorded in this repository, except the short "spoken" section, which is the everyday Hindi
# vocabulary of a workshop conversation.
#
# Format: one term per line, blank lines and "#" comments ignored, case as you would like it read
# back. A term must be at most 50 characters and at most 5 words — ElevenLabs rejects longer ones.
# Add a term by adding a line; no code change is needed.
#
# ORDER MATTERS. Deepgram caps keyterm prompting at 500 tokens and its own guidance is 20-50
# terms, past which the boost dilutes, so only the first terms are sent to it. Put the words a
# general model has no chance of guessing at the top and the ones it might already know at the
# bottom, and the truncation costs the least.
